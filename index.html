About

I work at the intersection of product management and AI governance in regulated banking. The hard problem is shipping AI that risk, compliance, and regulators actually trust.

My focus is turning governance and model-risk requirements into product: controls, data-quality guardrails, and observability that let AI move from pilot to production without breaking regulatory obligations. I translate dense regulatory expectations into product requirements, acceptance criteria, and controls that engineering and risk teams can both sign off on. Regulation on one side, AI systems on the other, that's where I'm strongest.

Outside the day job, I'm a hands-on AI builder. I ship consumer and civic AI products end to end: user research, PRD, LLM-backed features, data model, product analytics, and deploy, using agentic coding tools like Claude Code and Codex to go from a blank repo to a live URL in days, to stay fluent in the thing I govern.
